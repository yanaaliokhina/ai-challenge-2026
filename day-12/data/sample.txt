Embeddings are dense vector representations of text produced by neural networks.
They capture semantic meaning so that similar texts are close together in vector space.
Cosine similarity measures the angle between two vectors and is commonly used for semantic search.
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) combines a retrieval step with a language model generation step.
In RAG, relevant documents are fetched from an index and injected into the model prompt as context.
Vector databases store embeddings and support fast approximate nearest-neighbor search at scale.
Chunking is the process of splitting large documents into smaller pieces before embedding.
Each chunk should be semantically coherent to produce useful embeddings for retrieval.
JSON is a lightweight format for persisting structured data such as an embedding index.
Persistent indexes allow retrieval systems to avoid re-embedding content on every run.
